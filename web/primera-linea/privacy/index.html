Primera Línea privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Primera Línea is a logic puzzle for iPhone and Android. You can play without giving us any data: there are no accounts or sign-up, and your progress stays on your device. Only if you join the optional weekly marathon leaderboard, your alias and your best run of the week reach us (section 3.2). We fund it with ads served by Google and with an optional purchase to remove them. This page explains what data is collected, why, and how you decide. Where the text says "iPhone", Android works the same way with your Android phone and Google Play; only section 6 (measuring installs from ads) is Android-only.

3. Data that stays on your device

The game saves in the app's storage, not on any server:

  • Progress: levels completed, medals, stamps, skins, beaches visited and the daily challenge.
  • Times: how long you take on each level and your best time.
  • Settings: sound, volumes and similar options.
  • Hints and ads: the hints you have left, how many you got today (with and without an ad), the time of your last hint, how many levels since the last ad between levels, the time of the latest ads and the details of the last ad that opened (for "Report an ad", section 4.1). This is used to apply the limits in section 4.1, which are the same for everyone in your age group.
  • Stuck signal: the game works out on your device whether you have been on a level for a long time, to offer you a free hint, with no ad. It is never used to show you more ads and never leaves your device.
  • Daily reminder: if you turn it on in its full mode, the time of your first game each day (for the last 14 days), to notify you at your usual time. Notifications are local: your device schedules them, with no server involved. If you change mode, those times are deleted.

Legal basis: it is necessary for the game to work as you ask (GDPR Article 6.1.b), and storing it on the device is strictly necessary for the service (Spanish LSSI Article 22.2). We have no access to this data.

How long: until you delete the app. If you uninstall it, everything is deleted. If you use iCloud or Android backup, the platform may include this data in the backup according to your settings.

Sharing results: if you tap "Share", your system's share sheet opens with some text. You choose where to send it; we receive nothing.

3.1 App updates (Expo)

When the app opens, the game asks Expo (EAS Update) whether there is a content update and downloads it. In that request Expo receives a random installation identifier (it is not your advertising ID or your name), the app and system version, and your IP address like any web server. We do not use it to know who you are or for advertising. Legal basis: it is necessary for the app to work and stay up to date (GDPR Article 6.1.b and 6.1.f). Provider: Expo (USA); its policy is at expo.dev/privacy.

3.2 Weekly marathon leaderboard

The weekly leaderboard is optional and off by default. It is only for people who have said they are 14 or over (section 8). If you do not tap "Join", nothing of yours is sent; you can look at the list without joining. If you join, the app sends to our server (a Cloudflare service):

  • A random identifier created only for the leaderboard (it is not your advertising ID, your vendor ID, your name or your Apple ID).
  • Your alias, which anyone looking at the leaderboard will see. The app suggests one and you can change it once an hour. Do not use your real name: we never ask for it. We check that nobody else has it and that it does not contain certain offensive words.
  • Your best marathon run of each week: the boards solved and their solution (to check they are real), the attempt number, the time played, the spare time at the end and the ice creams you used and on which board. The list shows, next to your alias, the boards, the time and the ice creams.
  • If you choose one, the badge you want to show next to your alias.
  • When each run starts, the app asks for a seal: we sign it with the start time, your identifier, the week and the attempt, and it is used to check that the result is possible.

On the server we keep your identifier, your alias and when you set it, the badge, how many weeks you have been first (the crown), and for each week your best result with the time it arrived. We do not send your age, your location, your advertising ID, your campaign progress or anything about your purchases. Cloudflare sees your IP address to serve the request; we do not store it: only a hashed, shortened summary of it, to limit abuse (requests per hour), which is deleted within a week at most. The server keeps no access logs.

Why and legal basis: to show the leaderboard, your rank and the week's crown, and to prevent cheating and abuse. Basis: it is the service you ask for when you join (GDPR article 6.1.b); preventing cheating and abuse is our legitimate interest in a fair list and a service that holds up (article 6.1.f).

How long: every Monday, results and crowns older than 8 weeks are deleted automatically, and with them your alias if you have no results left. If you stop playing or delete the app without deleting your data, it disappears this way, at most about 9 weeks after your last result.

Where: Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) hosts the server as a processor. The data may be processed outside the European Economic Area; Cloudflare relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Its policy is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Your rights: in Leaderboard > My alias and settings you can change your alias, tap "Stop sending my results" (what was already sent stays until it expires or you delete it) or tap "Delete my data": your alias, results, crowns and badge are removed from the server at once. You can also write to hola@mosaicolab.app; since there is no account, we will ask for your alias to find your data.

4. Ads

4.1 When there are ads

  • Rewarded (you choose): when you run out of hints and ask for one, the game asks "Watch an ad to get 1 hint?", with "Yes" and "No, thanks". Each ad gives 1 hint. In the campaign there are at least 45 seconds between one hint and the next, and a maximum of 30 hints a day. In the daily challenge, which expires at midnight, there is no daily maximum and those hints do not count towards the campaign's. If you close it before it ends, you get no hint but that attempt is not used up. If the ad fails or does not load, we give you the hint anyway.
  • Ages 14 to 17: at most 5 hints a day come with an ad. The rest are given without an ad, with the same rules (45 seconds between hints and 30 a day).
  • Between levels: from level 10, after completing a campaign level a full-screen ad may appear, at most one every 2 levels and with at least 2 minutes since the previous ad (including the last rewarded one). Your progress is saved first. You first see the celebration and an "Ad in 3 s" notice; when you close the ad, you see your result.
  • In marathon mode: after completing the 5th, 10th, 15th... board, a full-screen ad may appear, at most one every 5 boards and respecting the same 2 minutes since the previous ad (if they have not passed, it waits for the next multiple of 5). You first see the celebration and the "Ad in 3 s" notice, and the marathon clock is paused while it lasts. Your progress is saved first. The same exclusions as in the campaign apply: it does not appear if, by your year of birth, you may be under 14, if you have not answered the age question, or if you have bought "Remove ads" (section 5).
  • There are never ads in the middle of a level that you did not ask for, when opening the game, when replaying a level you already completed, or on arriving at a beach or on journeys. In the daily challenge there are no ads between levels. There are no banners.
  • The same for everyone in your age group: when an ad appears does not depend on how well or badly you play, how much you play or any other data about you. It only changes with your age (section 8).
  • There are no ads at all if you bought "Remove ads" (section 5), if you said you are under 14, or if you have not answered the age question yet (section 8). In those cases, hints that would otherwise need an ad are given without one, with the same rules of 45 seconds and 30 a day.
  • An inappropriate ad? Use Settings > Ads > Report an ad or write to hola@mosaicolab.app, with a screenshot if you can, and we will block it. The button opens your email app with a message that already includes the date and time of the last ad, its type, its AdMob identifier, the network that served it and the game version, so we can find it. It only reaches us if you send the email; we use that data only to handle your report (GDPR Article 6.1.f: interest in removing inappropriate ads). On Google ads you can also tap their information icon to see why you are shown them.

4.3 What data they collect

It is collected by Google's SDK inside the app, not by our code. According to Google, it may collect:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising ID (IDFA on iPhone, Android advertising ID on Android), only if you are 18 or over and allow it where the system asks, and the vendor identifier (IDFV) on iPhone.
  • Approximate location inferred from your IP address (city or region). Never GPS: the app does not ask for location permission.
  • Technical device data: model, operating system version, language and connection type.
  • Ad data: which ads you have seen, whether you closed them and whether you tapped them.
  • Diagnostic data: crashes and performance of the SDK itself.

What our code never sends to Google: your progress, your times, your streak, the time you play, the stuck signal, your age or whether you have bought anything. (Our own server only receives your best marathon run if you join the leaderboard; section 3.2.) To Google we only tell, with each request, the maximum content rating of the ads and whether they must be non-personalised (section 8).

5. "Remove ads" purchase

If you buy "Remove ads" (a one-time purchase, at the price your store shows you):

  • It is handled by Apple or Google Play with their payment system. We do not see your name, your account or your payment details. For payment, the controller is the store (apple.com/legal/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy).
  • The game stores on your device that the purchase is done and checks it with the store, also when you tap "Restore purchases".
  • From then on no ads are requested or shown, and on later launches the game no longer starts Google's ads SDK, so it collects no advertising data either.
  • Hints that asked for an ad are given without one, with the same rules as section 4.1 (45 seconds between hints and 30 a day in the campaign).
  • We do not tell anyone, including Google, that you bought it.
  • Legal basis: fulfilling what you bought (GDPR Article 6.1.b). Refunds: handled by the store (Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com).

6. Analytics and measuring installs from ads (Android only)

Inside the app we use no behavioural analytics: we send no usage statistics, levels, purchases or progress to anyone. If we ever add it, we will say so here first, change the date above and, if needed, ask your permission in the game.

The only exception is measuring the installs that come from our ads, which works like this:

  • Scope: Android only and only if you are 18 or over and have given your consent. It does not exist on iPhone. If you said you are under 18, or if you have not answered yet, the measurement program is not even loaded.
  • Who receives the data: AppsFlyer, which provides this service to us, and, through AppsFlyer, TikTok and Meta (Facebook and Instagram), the networks where you may have seen our ad. We send it to no other partner.
  • What data: the Android advertising ID, the Google Play install referrer (which ad or link you came from), your IP address, the device model and system version, the language and the country. No in-game events are sent: no purchases, levels, times, hints or your age.
  • Why: only to measure the installs that come from ads and know which ads work, so that TikTok and Meta know whether an install came from their network. We do not use it to profile you or to show you more ads.
  • Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Article 6.1.a and Spanish LSSI Article 22.2). It is the same consent you give in the consent form in section 7: if you do not accept personalised ads, the program does not start.
  • How to withdraw consent: in game Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy options. When you withdraw it, or change your age answer to an under-18 group, the measurement program stops and sends nothing more. What was already sent cannot be recovered from the app; to ask for access to it or its deletion you can write to us or contact those companies (section 9).
  • Transfers outside the European Economic Area: AppsFlyer, TikTok and Meta may process data outside the EEA, including the United States. According to them, they rely on European Commission adequacy decisions, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.
  • Their own controllers: AppsFlyer acts on our behalf, but TikTok and Meta may act as controllers in their own right for the data they receive, under their own policies.
  • How long: we do not keep this data individually. Each company keeps it under its own policy.

Privacy policies: AppsFlyer, TikTok and Meta.

7. How you decide: consent and ATT

The first time you reach a level where hints are counted, the game asks your age (section 8). What follows depends on your age.

If you are 18 or over

  1. Google's consent form (UMP), a Google-certified, TCF-compatible consent management platform. "Accept" and "Reject" carry the same weight, and you can choose by purpose and by partner.
  2. If you accept personalised ads, a short screen explains what comes next and then, on iPhone, Apple's prompt "Allow the app to track your activity" (App Tracking Transparency) appears. If you reject in the form, that prompt does not appear.

If you are 14 to 17

  • You see the same Google consent form, but you do not see the previous screen or Apple's tracking prompt: we do not ask you.
  • Your ads are always non-personalised, even if you accept in the form. We do not use profile-based advertising with minors (Digital Services Act Article 28). Install measurement (section 6) does not apply to you.

For everyone

  • If you do not allow tracking, the game works the same and ads are not personalised.
  • If you reject in the form, Google may show you non-personalised ads (based on content, not on you) or limited ads, which according to Google use no identifiers and store no information on your device for advertising. If what you choose means ads cannot be requested, there are none, and hints that would ask for an ad are given without one, with the same rules as section 4.1. Rejecting never leaves you worse off in the game.
  • You can change your mind whenever you like: in game Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy options you reopen the form, and in your system settings (on iPhone, Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking) you change the platform permission. Before each ad, the game re-reads what you chose. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done with it before. On Android, withdrawing it also stops the install measurement in section 6.
  • Outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google does not show the form; the age rules in this section still apply.

8. Children

Primera Línea is rated 13+ on the App Store, because of its ads. It is not aimed at children and is not in the Kids category.

  • The age question: we ask "How old are you?" with three equal buttons: "18 or over", "14 to 17" and "Under 14". Only the group you pick is stored, and only on your device.
  • Under 14 or no answer: no ads program is started: no form, no tracking prompt, no ads. Hints that would otherwise ask for an ad are given without one, with the same rules as section 4.1 (45 seconds between hints and 30 a day in the campaign).
  • 14 to 17: no tracking prompt, always non-personalised ads (section 7) and at most 5 hints a day with an ad; the rest, without an ad and with the same rules (section 4.1).
  • In Spain, children under 14 cannot consent by themselves to the processing of their data (LOPDGDD Article 7).
  • Changing your answer: in Settings > Privacy you can answer if you have not yet or, if you already did, use "Change my age answer". If you have any problem, write to us and we will help.
  • Ad content: we ask Google for a content ceiling by age, on its own scale: for 14 to 17, at most PG (may require parental guidance); from 18, at most T (for teens). They are not "all audiences" ads: that would be Google's G category, which we do not use as a ceiling. We also block categories such as gambling, alcohol, dating or loans. Google's filter is not perfect: if you see something inappropriate, tell us (section 4.1).
  • Purchases: minors' purchases may need parental approval through Apple Family Sharing's "Ask to Buy" or Google Play's family controls.
  • Weekly leaderboard: only offered, and only sends data, if you have said you are 14 or over. With under 14, "Prefer not to say" or no answer, nothing is sent. If you change your year to under 14 after joining, the app stops sending; to delete what was already sent, write to us (or wait for it to expire after 8 weeks). Your alias is public: do not use your real name (section 3.2).
  • If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child's data has been processed, write to the email in section 1.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and port your data, to restrict its processing, to object to it and to withdraw your consent (GDPR Articles 15 to 22).

  • Data on your device: you control it and it is deleted when you uninstall the app. Since it does not reach us, we cannot view or delete it for you.
  • Ads data: withdraw consent in game Settings > Privacy, turn off tracking in your system settings and, for what was already collected, contact Google (links in section 4.2). If you write to us, we will help.
  • Install measurement (Android): withdraw consent as explained in section 6 and, for what was already sent, contact AppsFlyer, TikTok or Meta. If you write to us, we will help.
  • Leaderboard data: delete it at once with Leaderboard > My alias and settings > "Delete my data" or write to us with your alias (section 3.2).
  • Purchase data: held by the store; you can exercise your rights with them.
  • How to exercise them: write to hola@mosaicolab.app. We reply within one month at most.
  • Complaints: if you think we have not handled this properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

We only keep on our server the leaderboard data of people who join (section 3.2), with no accounts or contact details, and it travels encrypted (HTTPS). The rest of the game's data never leaves your device and is protected by its security. The ads and measurement programs send their data encrypted (HTTPS).

11. Changes to this policy

If we change something important (for example, a new ad network or analytics), we will update this page, change the date above and, if your consent is needed, ask for it again in the game.
