Primera Línea privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Primera Línea is a logic puzzle for iPhone and Android. You can play without giving us any data: there are no accounts or sign-up, and your progress stays on your device. Only if you join the optional weekly marathon leaderboard do we receive your alias, your best run of each week and your league rewards (section 3.2). We fund it with ads served by Google and with an optional purchase to remove them. This page explains what data is collected, why, and how you decide. Where the text says "iPhone", Android works the same way with your Android phone and Google Play; only section 6 (measuring installs from ads) is Android-only.

3. Data that stays on your device

The game saves in the app's storage, not on any server:

  • Progress: levels completed, medals, stamps, skins, beaches visited and the daily challenge.
  • Times: how long you take on each level and your best time.
  • Settings: sound, volumes and similar options.
  • Hints and ads: the hints you have left, how many you got today (with and without an ad), the time of your last hint, how many levels since the last ad between levels, the time of the latest ads and the details of the last ad that opened (for "Report an ad", section 4.1). This is used to apply the limits in section 4.1, which are the same for everyone.
  • Stuck signal: the game works out on your device whether you have been on a level for a long time, to offer you a free hint, with no ad. It is never used to show you more ads and never leaves your device.
  • Daily reminder: if you turn it on in its full mode, the time of your first game each day (for the last 14 days), to notify you at your usual time. Notifications are local: your device schedules them, with no server involved. If you change mode, those times are deleted.

Legal basis: it is necessary for the game to work as you ask (GDPR Article 6.1.b), and storing it on the device is strictly necessary for the service (Spanish LSSI Article 22.2). We have no access to this data.

How long: until you delete the app. If you uninstall it, everything is deleted. If you use iCloud or Android backup, the platform may include this data in the backup according to your settings.

Sharing results: if you tap "Share", your system's share sheet opens with some text. You choose where to send it; we receive nothing.

3.1 App updates (Expo)

On iPhone, the App Store version does not check for or download over-the-air updates: what you install is exactly what Apple reviewed, and Expo receives nothing. On Android, when the app opens, the game asks Expo (EAS Update) whether there is a content update and downloads it. In that request Expo receives a random installation identifier (it is not your advertising ID or your name), the app and system version, and your IP address like any web server. We do not use it to know who you are or for advertising. Legal basis: it is necessary for the app to work and stay up to date (GDPR Article 6.1.b and 6.1.f). Provider: Expo (USA); its policy is at expo.dev/privacy.

3.2 Weekly marathon leaderboard

The weekly leaderboard is optional and off by default. It is only for people who have said they are 14 or over (section 8). If you do not tap "Join", nothing of yours is sent; you can look at the list without joining. If you join, the app sends to our server (a Cloudflare service):

  • A random identifier created only for the leaderboard (it is not your advertising ID, your vendor ID, your name or your Apple ID).
  • Your alias, which other players will see (see "What others see" below). It comes from a list in the app (for example, "Faro Valiente"): nobody types their own, so it never contains your name. If you don't like it, you can ask for another one from the list once an hour. If someone else already has it, a number is added.
  • Your best marathon run of each week. To check it is real and work out the points, the app sends each board you clear with its solution, how long it took you, the floats you had left and the ones you lost, whether you used any help or tried again, and the ice creams you had and where they came from. The server replays the run, works out the points and doesn't keep the board-by-board detail, only the summary explained below.
  • If you pick one, the cap you want to show next to your alias.
  • When each run starts, the app asks for a seal: we sign it with the start time, your identifier, the week and the attempt, and it lets us check the result is possible.
  • While you play, the app may ask who is just ahead of you, sending your points and time at that moment. This isn't stored.

What we store:

  • Your identifier, your alias and when you set it, your cap and the last time the app connected to the leaderboard.
  • For each week, your best score: points, boards played and cleared, total time, highest difficulty, how many times you used help, your starting and cooler ice creams, when it arrived and whether the server flagged it as doubtful.
  • Your league rewards: starfish (and the league they give you), how many times you finished 1st, 2nd or 3rd, and the weeks you wore the crown.
  • Anti-cheat notes: if a score looks doubtful or someone wins the crown, we keep a note with your identifier, the week, the reason, your points, the boards played and the time since the run started. At most one per reason and week. It doesn't include your alias or IP. Only our team sees it, to check doubtful podiums by hand, and it isn't used for anything else.

We don't send your age, location, advertising ID, campaign progress or anything about your purchase. Cloudflare sees your IP address to handle the request, but we don't store it: only a shortened, hashed summary used to limit abuse (requests per hour), deleted within a week at the latest. The server keeps no access logs.

What others see:

  • On the week's list (the top 50), people see your alias, cap, points, boards, time, ice creams and, if you wear it, the crown; if you are in the top three, also the podium frame. The list also sends other players' apps your league, the highest difficulty in your run and how many times you used help.
  • Also, any player who has joined can see the players just ahead of them, even outside the top 50. If you are ahead of someone, they can see your alias, cap, league, points, time and position.
  • If you typed your own alias before 17 November 2026, it is no longer shown: one from the list is shown instead.
  • Your identifier is never shown.

Doubtful scores: if a run looks impossible (for example, times faster than a person can play or points far outside the normal range), it stays on the list but doesn't count for the podium, the crown or that week's starfish. The server decides this with the same rules for everyone. If you think it's a mistake, write to us and we'll check it by hand.

Why and legal basis:

  • To show the leaderboard, your position, who is ahead of you, the podium, the crown and your league. Basis: it is the service you ask for when you join (GDPR Article 6.1.b).
  • To prevent cheating and abuse. Basis: our legitimate interest in a fair list and a service that holds up (Article 6.1.f).

How long:

  • Each week's runs, podiums and crowns are deleted automatically after 8 weeks.
  • Anti-cheat notes are deleted automatically within 2 weeks at the latest.
  • Your alias, cap and league rewards (starfish, league, podiums and crowns won) are kept while you keep using the leaderboard, even if you stop playing: you don't lose starfish for not playing. The app connects on its own when you open it with the leaderboard turned on, when you look at the list and when you play the marathon.
  • If 12 months go by without the app connecting with your identifier, they are deleted completely, with no anonymous copy left. If you have no rewards, your alias is deleted sooner: 8 weeks after your last score.
  • If you delete the app without tapping "Delete my data", your alias stops appearing on the list within 8 weeks at most, and the rest is deleted after 12 months.
  • Cloudflare's technical backups (which let the server be restored to how it was at an earlier moment) may keep deleted data for up to 30 more days.

Where: Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) hosts the server as a processor. The data may be processed outside the European Economic Area; Cloudflare relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Its policy is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Your rights: in Leaderboard > My alias and settings you can:

  • change your alias for another one from the list;
  • tap "Stop sending my scores": what was already sent stays until it expires as explained above, or until you delete it;
  • tap "Delete my data": your alias, scores, starfish, podiums, crowns, cap and any anti-cheat notes are deleted from the server straight away.

You can also write to hola@mosaicolab.app. As there is no account, we'll ask for your alias to find your data.

4. Ads

4.1 When there are ads

  • Rewarded (you choose): when you run out of hints and ask for one, the game asks "Watch an ad to get 2 hints?", with "Yes" and "No, thanks". Each ad gives 2 hints: you use one straight away and the other is saved. In the campaign there are at least 10 seconds between one hint and the next. There is no daily maximum. In the daily challenge, which expires at midnight, there is no wait between hints either. If you close it before it ends, you get no hint but that attempt is not used up. If the ad fails or does not load, we give you the hint anyway.
  • At most 5 hints a day come with an ad. The rest are given without an ad, with the same rules (10 seconds between hints in the campaign and no daily maximum).
  • Between levels: from level 10, after completing a campaign level a full-screen ad may appear, at most one every 2 levels and with at least 3 minutes since the previous ad (including the last rewarded one). Your progress is saved first. You first see the celebration and an "Ad in 3 s" notice; when you close the ad, you see your result.
  • In Lost objects: from the 3rd puzzle, tapping "Next" may show a full-screen ad, at most one every 2 puzzles and with the same 3 minutes since the previous ad, after the "Ad in 3 s" notice. Never in the first puzzle or while digging up an object, and with the same exclusions as in the campaign.
  • In marathon mode: after completing the 5th, 10th, 15th... board, a full-screen ad may appear, at most one every 5 boards and respecting the same 3 minutes since the previous ad (if they have not passed, it waits for the next multiple of 5). You first see the celebration and the "Ad in 3 s" notice, and the marathon clock is paused while it lasts. Your progress is saved first. The same exclusions as in the campaign apply: it does not appear if you have bought "Remove ads" (section 5) or if you said you were under 14 in an earlier version (section 8).
  • There are never ads in the middle of a level that you did not ask for, when opening the game, when replaying a level you already completed, or on arriving at a beach or on journeys. In the daily challenge there are no ads between levels. There are no banners.
  • The same for everyone: when an ad appears does not depend on how well or badly you play, how much you play or any other data about you.
  • There are no ads at all if you bought "Remove ads" (section 5) or if you said you were under 14 in an earlier version (section 8). In those cases, hints that would otherwise need an ad are given without one, with the same 10-second wait and no daily maximum.
  • An inappropriate ad? Use Settings > Ads > Report an ad or write to hola@mosaicolab.app, with a screenshot if you can, and we will block it. The button opens your email app with a message that already includes the date and time of the last ad, its type, its AdMob identifier, the network that served it and the game version, so we can find it. It only reaches us if you send the email; we use that data only to handle your report (GDPR Article 6.1.f: interest in removing inappropriate ads). On Google ads you can also tap their information icon to see why you are shown them.

4.3 What data they collect

It is collected by Google's SDK inside the app, not by our code. According to Google, it may collect:

  • Device identifiers: we do not request or use the advertising ID (IDFA) or show Apple's tracking prompt. Google may collect the vendor identifier (IDFV).
  • Approximate location inferred from your IP address (city or region). Never GPS: the app does not ask for location permission.
  • Technical device data: model, operating system version, language and connection type.
  • Ad data: which ads you have seen, whether you closed them and whether you tapped them.
  • Diagnostic data: crashes and performance of the SDK itself.

What our code never sends to Google: your progress, your times, your streak, the time you play, the stuck signal, your age or whether you have bought anything. (Our own server only receives your best marathon run if you join the leaderboard; section 3.2.) To Google we only tell, with each request, the maximum content rating of the ads and whether they must be non-personalised (section 8).

5. "Remove ads" purchase

If you buy "Remove ads" (a one-time purchase, at the price your store shows you):

  • It is handled by Apple or Google Play with their payment system. We do not see your name, your account or your payment details. For payment, the controller is the store (apple.com/legal/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy).
  • The game stores on your device that the purchase is done and checks it with the store, also when you tap "Restore purchases".
  • From then on no ads are requested or shown, and on later launches the game no longer starts Google's ads SDK, so it collects no advertising data either.
  • Hints that asked for an ad are given without one, with the same rules as section 4.1 (10 seconds between hints in the campaign and no daily maximum).
  • We do not tell anyone, including Google, that you bought it.
  • Legal basis: fulfilling what you bought (GDPR Article 6.1.b). Refunds: handled by the store (Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com).

6. Analytics and measuring installs from ads (Android only)

Inside the app we use no behavioural analytics: we send no usage statistics, levels, purchases or progress to anyone. If we ever add it, we will say so here first, change the date above and, if needed, ask your permission in the game.

The only exception is measuring the installs that come from our ads, which works like this:

  • Scope: Android only and only if you are 18 or over and have given your consent. It does not exist on iPhone. In Android versions that still ask your age it is only active if you said 18 or over; in versions that no longer ask, it is not active for anyone.
  • Who receives the data: AppsFlyer, which provides this service to us, and, through AppsFlyer, TikTok and Meta (Facebook and Instagram), the networks where you may have seen our ad. We send it to no other partner.
  • What data: the Android advertising ID, the Google Play install referrer (which ad or link you came from), your IP address, the device model and system version, the language and the country. No in-game events are sent: no purchases, levels, times, hints or your age.
  • Why: only to measure the installs that come from ads and know which ads work, so that TikTok and Meta know whether an install came from their network. We do not use it to profile you or to show you more ads.
  • Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Article 6.1.a and Spanish LSSI Article 22.2). It is the same consent you give in the consent form in section 7: if you do not accept personalised ads, the program does not start.
  • How to withdraw consent: in game Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy options. When you withdraw it, the measurement program stops and sends nothing more. What was already sent cannot be recovered from the app; to ask for access to it or its deletion you can write to us or contact those companies (section 9).
  • Transfers outside the European Economic Area: AppsFlyer, TikTok and Meta may process data outside the EEA, including the United States. According to them, they rely on European Commission adequacy decisions, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.
  • Their own controllers: AppsFlyer acts on our behalf, but TikTok and Meta may act as controllers in their own right for the data they receive, under their own policies.
  • How long: we do not keep this data individually. Each company keeps it under its own policy.

Privacy policies: AppsFlyer, TikTok and Meta.

7. How you decide: consent and tracking

Primera Línea does not ask your age. Everyone gets the same treatment, the most protected one we offer:

  • We never ask for Apple's tracking permission ("Allow the app to track your activity") and we do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Your ads are always non-personalised, even if you accept in the form. We do not use profile-based advertising with minors (Digital Services Act Article 28).
  • Where the law requires it (in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland) you see Google's consent form (UMP). "Accept" and "Reject" carry the same weight.
  • If you reject in the form, Google may show you non-personalised ads (based on content, not on you) or limited ads, which according to Google use no identifiers and store no information on your device for advertising. If what you choose means ads cannot be requested, there are none, and hints that would ask for an ad are given without one, with the same rules as section 4.1. Rejecting never leaves you worse off in the game.
  • You can change your mind whenever you like: in game Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy options you reopen the form, and in your system settings (on iPhone, Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking) you change the platform permission. Before each ad, the game re-reads what you chose. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done with it before. On Android, withdrawing it also stops the install measurement in section 6.

8. Children

Primera Línea is rated 13+ on the App Store. It is not meant for children under 14, not aimed at children and not in the Kids category.

  • We do not ask your age. Since we do not know it, everyone gets the treatment of the most protected group: no tracking prompt, always non-personalised ads, a content ceiling of PG and at most 5 hints a day with an ad.
  • If in an earlier version you said you were under 14, we still load no ads program for you. That answer stays only on your device; to change it, reinstall the app or write to us.
  • Earlier versions (on iPhone up to build 17; on Android, until its next update): they ask your age and work as this policy described before 24/11/2026. If you have one, it keeps your answer.
  • In Spain, children under 14 cannot consent by themselves to the processing of their data (LOPDGDD Article 7).
  • Ad content: we ask Google for a content ceiling, on its own scale: at most PG (may require parental guidance). They are not "all audiences" ads: that would be Google's G category, which we do not use as a ceiling. We also block categories such as gambling, alcohol, dating or loans. Google's filter is not perfect: if you see something inappropriate, tell us (section 4.1).
  • Weekly leaderboard: only if you tick "I am 14 or over". It is your own declaration and we do not check it. If you are under 14, do not tick it. If you think a minor has joined, write to us and we will delete their data. Your alias is public and comes from a list, so it never contains your name (section 3.2).
  • Purchases: minors' purchases may need parental approval through Apple Family Sharing's "Ask to Buy" or Google Play's family controls.
  • If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child's data has been processed, write to the email in section 1.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and port your data, to restrict its processing, to object to it and to withdraw your consent (GDPR Articles 15 to 22).

  • Data on your device: you control it and it is deleted when you uninstall the app. Since it does not reach us, we cannot view or delete it for you.
  • Ads data: withdraw consent in game Settings > Privacy, turn off tracking in your system settings and, for what was already collected, contact Google (links in section 4.2). If you write to us, we will help.
  • Install measurement (Android): withdraw consent as explained in section 6 and, for what was already sent, contact AppsFlyer, TikTok or Meta. If you write to us, we will help.
  • Leaderboard data: delete it at once with Leaderboard > My alias and settings > "Delete my data" or write to us with your alias (section 3.2).
  • Purchase data: held by the store; you can exercise your rights with them.
  • How to exercise them: write to hola@mosaicolab.app. We reply within one month at most.
  • Complaints: if you think we have not handled this properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

We only keep on our server the leaderboard data of people who join (section 3.2), with no accounts or contact details, and it travels encrypted (HTTPS). The rest of the game's data never leaves your device and is protected by its security. The ads and measurement programs send their data encrypted (HTTPS).

11. Changes to this policy

If we change something important (for example, a new ad network or analytics), we will update this page, change the date above and, if your consent is needed, ask for it again in the game.
