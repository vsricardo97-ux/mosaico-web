Tesela Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Tesela is a daily logic puzzle app. You can play it without giving us any data: there are no accounts or sign-up, and your progress stays on your phone. We fund it with ads served by Google and with «No ads», an optional purchase that removes them. Here we explain what data is collected, what for, and how you decide.

1. Who is responsible

  • Data controller: Ricardo Verdú Sánchez (Mosaico Lab), Valencia, Spain
  • Privacy email: hola@mosaicolab.app
  • Website: https://mosaicolab.app

The rest of the owner's details are in the legal notice (in Spanish).

We have no data protection officer because we are not required to (Article 37 GDPR and Article 34 of Spain's LOPDGDD). For anything about your data, email us at the address above.

3. Data that stays on your phone

The app stores in its own storage, not on any server:

  • Progress: puzzles solved in each game (Zonas, Camino, Binario and Flota), days played, the tiles and mosaics in your collection, the technique notebook and puzzles you left unfinished.
  • Times: how long each puzzle takes you, if you keep the timer visible.
  • Settings: sound, timer, appearance and similar options.
  • Hints and ads: the hints you have left, how many you got today (with and without an ad), the time of your last hint, how many puzzles you have finished, the time of the latest ads and the details of the last ad that opened (for «Report an ad», section 4.1). This is used to apply the limits in section 4.1, which are the same for everyone in your age group.
  • Stuck signal: the app works out on your phone whether you have been on a puzzle for a long time, to offer you a free hint, without an ad. It is never used to show you more ads and it never leaves your phone.
  • Usage statistics: the app adds up, on your phone, how many puzzles you finish (by mode and game), how long your sessions last and how many ads are offered, accepted and shown. They are totals only, with no dates, identifiers or device data, and they never leave your phone.
  • Daily reminder: after you solve your first daily challenge, the app asks once whether you want to allow its notifications. If you do, it stores the time you usually play so it can remind you at that time, at most once a day and never at night. Notifications are local: your phone schedules them, without any server. You can turn them off in Tesela's settings or in the system settings.
  • Purchases: if you have «No ads», the type of purchase, its status and its renewal or trial end date, so it works without a connection (section 5).

Legal basis: it is what the app needs to work as you ask (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), and storing it on the device is strictly necessary for the service (Article 22(2) of Spain's LSSI, which implements the ePrivacy Directive). We have no access to this data.

How long: until you delete the app. If you uninstall it, everything is deleted. If you use iCloud backup, Apple may include this data according to your settings.

Sharing results: if you tap «Share», the system share sheet opens with a text (and, if you choose it, an image made on your phone) showing the games you solved that day and, if visible, your time. The link in the text carries nothing that identifies you. You choose where to send it; we receive nothing.

4. Ads

4.1 When there are ads

  • Rewarded (you choose them): in your first puzzles of each game, hints are not counted. After that you have a hint counter that refills on its own. If you run out and ask for a hint, the app asks «Watch an ad to get 1 hint?», with «Yes» and «No, thanks». Each ad gives 1 hint. There are at least 45 seconds between one hint and the next, with a maximum of 30 hints a day in free play and the archive. In the daily challenge there is no daily maximum, and those hints do not count towards the other modes. If you close the ad before it ends, there is no hint, but that attempt is not used up either. If the ad fails or does not arrive, you get the hint anyway.
  • Ages 14 to 17: at most 5 hints a day come with an ad. The rest are given without an ad.
  • Between free-play puzzles: from your 10th finished puzzle, tapping «Next puzzle» may show a full-screen ad, with an «Ad in 3 s» notice and at least 2 minutes since the previous ad (including the last rewarded one). Your progress is saved first.
  • Fixed ad under the result (18 and over only): in free play, from your 10th puzzle, a static ad labelled «Ad» may appear under the result, never on the same puzzle as a full-screen ad.
  • Never are there ads in the middle of a puzzle unless you ask, when opening the app, in the daily challenge or in the archive (except the ones you choose for a hint). There are no banners.
  • The same for everyone in your age group: when an ad appears does not depend on how well or badly you play, how much you play or any other data about you. It only changes with your age (section 9).
  • There are no ads at all if you have «No ads» (section 5), if you are under the age of consent in your country, if you chose «I'd rather not say» or if you have not answered yet (section 9). In those cases, hints that would otherwise need an ad are given without one, with the same waits and limits.
  • An inappropriate ad? Use Settings > Ads > Report an ad or email hola@mosaicolab.app, with a screenshot if you can, and we will block it. The button opens your email app with a message that already includes the date and time of the last ad, its type, its AdMob identifier, the network that served it and the app version, so we can find it. It only reaches us if you send the email; we use that data only to handle your report (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: our interest in removing inappropriate ads). In Google's ads you can also tap the information icon to see why you are being shown it.

4.3 What data they collect

It is collected by Google's software (SDK) inside the app, not by our code. According to Google, it may collect:

  • Device identifiers: Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you are 18 or over and allow it in Apple's prompt, and the identifier for vendor (IDFV).
  • Approximate location inferred from your IP address (city or region). Never GPS: the app does not ask for location permission.
  • Technical device data: model, system version, language and connection type.
  • Ad data: which ads you saw, whether you closed them and whether you tapped them.
  • Diagnostic data: crashes and performance of the SDK itself.

What our code never sends: your progress, times, statistics, the time you play, the stuck signal, your age or whether you have bought anything. With each request we only tell Google the maximum content rating of the ads and whether they must be non-personalised (section 9).

5. «No ads»: purchases and subscriptions

«No ads» removes all ads. It can be an annual subscription (with a 7-day free trial, if the store offers you one), a monthly subscription or a one-time payment, at the prices the store shows you.

  • Payment is handled by Apple. We do not see your name, your Apple ID or your payment details. For the payment, Apple is the controller (apple.com/legal/privacy).
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States) checks your purchases for us as a processor. It receives a random identifier created by the app (not your Apple ID, and it does not identify your device), your purchase data (product, date, status, renewal and store country) and technical connection data (such as IP address, app and system version). We do not send it your email, your name, the advertising identifier or any game data, and automatic collection of device identifiers is turned off. Its policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.
  • When it connects: only if you are 14 or over according to the age question or the store, or if you tap «Restore purchases». It is also used to find out your store country, which sets the age of consent (section 9). Your location is never used.
  • The app stores your purchase status on your phone and checks it with the store, including when you tap «Restore purchases». With «No ads», on the following launches the app no longer starts Google's ad software, so no advertising data is collected either.
  • Trial end reminder: if you start the free trial, the app asks whether you want a reminder before the first charge. If you say yes, it schedules a local notification on your phone a few days before, with the date, the price and how to cancel. You will also see the same notice inside the app on those days.
  • Cancelling and refunds: in Settings > No ads > Manage subscription or in your Apple account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • We tell no one, including Google, that you have bought anything.
  • Legal basis: performing what you bought (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Transfers: RevenueCat processes data in the United States, with the safeguards of our processing agreement with it (the European Commission's standard contractual clauses).
  • How long: RevenueCat keeps the purchase history while we use its service, so you can restore your purchases, and deletes it when we ask.

6. Analytics

We use no third-party analytics: the app sends usage statistics to no one. The totals in section 3 stay on your phone. If we ever send them anywhere, we will say so here first, change the date above and, if needed, ask for your permission in the app.

7. App updates

When it opens, the app may check for a small update (for example, a fix) on the servers of Expo (650 Industries, Inc., United States), the service we build it with. That request carries technical data: IP address, platform, app version and a random installation identifier that is not linked to you. It is only used to download the update, never for advertising or analytics. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in the app working and being secure (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Its policy: expo.dev/privacy.

8. How you decide: consent and ATT

The age question (section 9) appears on the Home screen after you finish a few puzzles, never in the middle of one. What comes next depends on your age.

If you are 18 or over

  1. Google's consent form (UMP), a Google-certified consent management platform compatible with the TCF. «Accept» and «Reject» carry the same weight, and you can choose by purpose and by partner.
  2. If you accept personalised ads, a short screen explains what comes next, and then Apple's prompt «Allow the app to track your activity» appears (App Tracking Transparency). If you reject in the form, that prompt does not appear.

If you are 14 to 17 (and reach the age of consent in your country)

  • You see the same Google consent form, but not the preliminary screen or Apple's tracking prompt: we do not ask you.
  • Your ads are always non-personalised, even if you accept in the form, also outside Europe. We do not use profiling-based advertising with minors (Article 28 of the Digital Services Act).

For everyone

  • If you do not allow tracking, the app works the same and ads are not personalised.
  • If you reject in the form, Google may show you non-personalised ads (based on content, not on you) or limited ads, which according to Google use no identifiers and store no information on your phone for advertising. If your choices mean ads cannot be requested, there are none, and hints that needed an ad are given without one. Rejecting never leaves you worse off in the game.
  • You can change your mind at any time: in Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy options you reopen the form, and in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking you change Apple's permission. Before each ad, the app checks your choices again. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done before with it.
  • Outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google may not show the form; the age rules in this section still apply.

9. Minors and age

Tesela is rated 13+ on the App Store because of its ads. It is not directed at children and is not in the App Store's Kids category.

  • The age question: we ask for your birth year in a neutral way, without suggesting any answer. You can answer «I'd rather not say» or close it without answering. Only the year is stored, and only on your phone.
  • The store's age range: if your iPhone offers it (for example, if your family set it up with Family Sharing), the app asks Apple for your age range and, if Apple provides it, it takes precedence over our question. Apple only gives us the range (for example, «13 to 15»), not your date of birth, and the app stores it only on your phone. If your parents or guardians need to approve, or withdraw their permission, there are no ads and no purchases.
  • The age of consent in your country: to see ads or make purchases you must be at least the age at which your country lets you consent to the use of your data on your own, and never under 14 (for example, 14 in Spain, 15 in France and 16 in Germany). If we do not know your store country, we use 16.
  • Below that age, with «I'd rather not say» or with no answer: no ad or purchase software is started: no form, no tracking prompt, no ads and no RevenueCat (unless you tap «Restore purchases»). Hints that would otherwise need an ad are given without one.
  • Ages 14 to 17: no Apple tracking prompt, always non-personalised ads (section 8), no fixed ad under the result and at most 5 hints a day with an ad. For purchases, only the one-time payment from Settings: no subscriptions or free trials.
  • How we count age: since the year alone does not tell us your exact date, we count the age you are sure to have (the age you had on 1 January this year). If you reach the age of a group this year, we treat you as the previous group until the following 1 January. After that it changes on its own.
  • Changing your answer: in Settings > Privacy you can answer if you have not yet or, if you have, use «Change my year of birth». If you have any problem, email us and we will help.
  • Ad content: we ask Google for a content ceiling based on your age, on Google's own scale: ages 14 to 17, at most PG (parental guidance); 18 and over, at most T (teen). These are not «general audience» ads: that would be Google's G category, which we do not use as the ceiling. We also block categories such as gambling, alcohol, dating and loans. Google's filter is not perfect: if you see something inappropriate, let us know (section 4.1).
  • Purchases: purchases by minors may need parental approval through Apple's Family Sharing «Ask to Buy».
  • If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor's data has been processed, email us at the address in section 1.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and port your data, to restrict its processing, to object to it and to withdraw your consent (Articles 15 to 22 GDPR).

  • Data on your phone: you control it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app. Since it does not reach us, we cannot view or delete it for you.
  • Ad data: withdraw consent in Settings > Privacy, turn off tracking in iOS settings and, for data already collected, contact Google (links in section 4.2). If you email us, we will help.
  • Purchase data: payment data is held by Apple. For the data held by RevenueCat, email us and we will ask it to delete it (if it does, you may need to tap «Restore purchases» again).
  • How to exercise them: email hola@mosaicolab.app. We reply within one month at most.
  • Complaints: if you think we have not handled it well, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or to the data protection authority in your country.

11. If you live outside the European Union

  • United Kingdom: the UK GDPR applies, with the same rights as section 10. You can complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).
  • United States: we do not sell your data or share it for advertising in exchange for money. What Google's SDK collects for personalised ads is only used if you are 18 or over and agree. To opt out of personalised advertising, reject in the form (if shown) and do not allow tracking; if you need help, email us. Under-18s are never shown personalised ads, and the app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

12. Security

We have no servers with player data. The app's data is protected by your phone's security. The ad and purchase SDKs and the update check send their data encrypted (HTTPS).

13. Changes to this policy

If we change something important (for example, a new ad network or analytics), we will update this page, change the date above and, if your consent is needed, ask for it again in the app.
