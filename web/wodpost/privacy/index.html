Wodpost privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Wodpost reads the photo of your box's whiteboard, builds a workout card for your stories and keeps your WODs. There are no accounts and no sign-up: your history stays on your phone (for each board read in the cloud we keep the photo, the reading and your rating; if you're 18 or over, also a random app identifier and your approximate area, which you can delete in Settings; sections 4.4 and 4.5). We fund it with ads served by Google and with Premium, an optional purchase. This page explains what data is used, why, and how you decide.

  • Controller: Ricardo Verdú Sánchez (Mosaico Lab)
  • Privacy email: hola@mosaicolab.app
  • Website: https://mosaicolab.app

Other details are in the legal notice (in Spanish). We don't have a data protection officer because we are not required to (Article 37 GDPR).

3. Data that stays on your phone

  • Your workouts: WODs read or typed in, the ones you save "for another day", your results (times, rounds, loads), your notes and the date.
  • Weight (optional): only if you enter it to see estimated calories (a rough average, shown with “~” and “est.”). It's stored in the Keychain of this iPhone only: it doesn't go to iCloud backup or other devices. If you're 18 or over, it's also sent with your saved boards (see §4.5). You can delete it in Settings.
  • Settings and preferences: units, card style, the background you chose last time, the numbers you usually pick and similar.
  • Age: the band you choose (under 14, 14 to 17, or 18 or over). Only the band, never your date of birth.
  • Free readings: how many you have left today, in the iPhone's Keychain, so they're not lost if you reinstall.
  • Photos: the whiteboard photo is deleted from the phone when the reading ends. You choose the background photo or video from your camera roll or camera; the app uses a temporary copy while you build the card.
  • Videos you pick for "Edit it myself" (only in test versions for iPhone; section 4.9): the video is edited on your phone. The app reads only the videos you pick straight from your camera roll, without copying or uploading them. It asks for permission to access Photos to do this; if you don't give it, it reads no videos. The edited video is kept only on your phone, with its workout, until you delete it or tap "Delete my data". Nothing is uploaded unless you choose the AI edit (section 4.9).
  • Voice-over and your clips' sound in "Edit it myself" (only in test versions for iPhone): if you record your voice with the microphone or use your clips' original sound, everything is processed on your phone. The recording is kept only in the app while you edit the video, isn't sent to us or anyone else, and you can delete it from the editor. The app asks for microphone permission only when you tap "Record voice".
  • Automatic subtitles in "Edit it myself" (optional; only in test versions for iPhone): they use your phone's speech recognition, on the device only. The audio never leaves it and we don't keep what you say; the text only appears on the video you make on your phone. If your phone can't recognise that language offline, there are no subtitles: no cloud service is used. The app asks for speech recognition permission only when you tap "Subtitles". This is different from dictating your workout by voice (section 4.6), where the recording is sent to our service to turn it into text.

Legal basis: what's needed for the app to work as you ask (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); storing it on the device is strictly necessary for the service. We have no access to this data.

How long: until you delete it or delete the app. If you use iCloud backup, Apple may include your workouts in it depending on your settings (not your weight).

Sharing: if you share the card (to Instagram, through the share sheet or by saving it to Photos), you choose where it goes. When you share to Instagram, the image or video goes to Instagram through the iPhone's clipboard and Meta's policy applies. We receive nothing.

4. Reading the whiteboard in the cloud

4.1 What happens to the photo

Before the first reading we explain this and ask for your permission. When reading, Wodpost sends the whiteboard photo, downsized and without location or device data, to our service on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.), which forwards it to Anthropic (Anthropic, PBC) so its Claude AI model can read the workout. Our service keeps the photo, what was read and your rating to improve reading, without your name or account (sections 4.4 and 4.5). Anthropic only reads it: it does not train its models on it and deletes its copy within 30 days, unless it needs it longer to investigate a breach of its usage policy or the law requires it. Only the workout (sections, movements and numbers) becomes data: never other people's names or scores.

The board may show other people's names: that's why we ask you to crop to your workout. If your name is in a photo and you want to exercise your rights, write to us.

4.2 Legal basis and processors

  • Your photo: providing the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Other people's names that may appear on the board: our legitimate interest in reading your workout and improving reading with as little data as possible (Art. 6(1)(f)). They are never extracted or turned into data; they may still be visible in the kept photo (section 4.4).
  • Anthropic and Cloudflare act as processors under contract (Art. 28) and European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (and, for Cloudflare, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework) for the transfer to the United States. Policies: anthropic.com/legal/privacy and cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

4.3 How we count readings

  • Without Premium: you get 1 free reading a day and another for each rewarded video you watch (up to a daily maximum). To count them, the app creates a random installation ID (not your Apple ID, not the advertising ID, nothing about you) and keeps it in the iPhone's Keychain. Our service turns it into a hashed code with a key that changes every day and deletes everything at midnight (UTC), key included: we can't tell who you are or link one day to another.
  • Credits (without Premium, 14 or over): you can earn credits (up to 3) by watching a video or saving your workout of the day, and spend them on an extra reading (1 credit) or a Premium style (3 credits, at most one a week). To keep your balance we store on our service (Cloudflare) an encrypted code of your installation ID, the balance, the date of the last style paid with credits and an encrypted fingerprint of your last saved workout. To give you the daily credit we send your hand-written workout (movements, reps, load or time and format; never body weight or calories). We check it on the spot and don't keep it. If you don't use the app for 90 days, we delete it, and also when you tap «Delete my data». Legal basis: providing the service (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To prevent abuse we use Apple's DeviceCheck, which checks that the request comes from a real iPhone without identifying you.
  • With Premium: on iPhone the app sends Apple's signed purchase receipt; on Android, the identifier of your Google Play purchase (the "purchase token"). Our service checks it with Apple or Google Play and counts your readings for the day with the same hashed code, deleted at midnight.
  • Legal basis: providing the service (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); creating the ID and storing it on the iPhone is necessary for the service you ask for.

If you don't want to use the cloud, you can type your workout in yourself.

4.4 We keep your boards to improve reading

When you read a board in the cloud, we keep on our service (Cloudflare) the photo that was read (cropped, without location), what the app understood and the 1-5 rating you give the reading. We also note whether you took that board's card to Instagram, Share or Save (the button, the background type and the platform; never who you send it to). For people over 18 we also keep a copy of the final card, just the card without your background photo, next to that board, to see what you correct in the app's reading. We tell you in the app.

  • Without your name or account: we don't keep your name, account or IP. From under-18s, no identifier at all. If you're 18 or over, each board also carries what section 4.5 describes.
  • Why: to measure and improve reading and our products, and to produce aggregated, anonymous statistics (section 4.7). The stored data itself is only seen by our team. We may run some photos through Anthropic again to test improvements.
  • How long: there is no fixed deletion date: it is kept for as long as it serves those purposes, or until you ask us to delete it (section 10). If we ever wanted to use your data in another way, we'll ask you first.
  • Minors: we don't keep photos from under-18s, only what was read (no people).
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving reading (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), balanced against your rights: no name or account, cropped photo without location, nobody outside the team sees it and, if you're 18 or over, you can delete what links it to you (section 4.5).
  • If you don't want it kept: type the workout in instead of taking a photo. To ask us to delete a photo (for example, if your name is on it), write to us with the approximate date and time.
  • Reports and reviewed names: if you give 1 or 2 stars and tap «Send», we also get your corrections and comment. If you confirm or change the name of an uncertain movement, we only get that name. We keep them for as long as they help us improve reading; since they carry no identifier, to delete one write to us with the approximate date and time.

4.5 Identifier, area and calorie details (18 or over)

From the November 2026 version, if you're 18 or over, each board we keep (section 4.4) also carries:

  • The random installation identifier (the one in section 4.3): it isn't your Apple ID, your Google account or the advertising identifier. It links your boards together and lets us delete them if you ask.
  • Your approximate area: the country, region and city our provider (Cloudflare) works out from the connection (it may not be exact). Never GPS or the IP.
  • If you fill them in for calories and turn on "Send this data": weight, sex, birth year and how you train (starting out, often or competing). They're optional and, to estimate calories, they stay on your phone. Sending is a separate permission, off by default: if you don't turn it on or you turn calories off, they aren't sent.
  • Why: to improve reading and Wodpost (for example, which workouts and formats are most common by country, age or level) and to fine-tune calories. Individually, only our team sees this data: we don't sell or share it. It may be part of the aggregated, anonymous statistics in section 4.7. If we ever wanted to use your data in another way, we'll ask you first.
  • Legal basis: the identifier and area, our legitimate interest in improving the service (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), with the right to object and delete them any time. Weight, sex, birth year and level, your consent when you turn on "Send this data", with the notice next to it (Articles 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)), which you withdraw by turning it off or deleting them.
  • How long: for as long as it serves the purposes above, or until you delete it.
  • Delete my data: in Settings, "Delete my data". It removes the identifier, area and those details from all your boards; the board stays as in section 4.4, with nothing linking it to you. One tap, no questions.
  • Under 18: none of this is sent.

4.6 Dictating your workout by voice or text (optional)

If you choose "By voice or text", you can record up to 90 seconds with the microphone or type the workout. The recording is sent to our service on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.), which turns it into text with a speech recognition model run by Cloudflare itself (Workers AI). We don't keep the audio: it's deleted from your phone once sent and our service doesn't store it. The text appears in the app for you to correct and, when you tap "Fill in the workout", it's sent to Anthropic (Anthropic, PBC), whose Claude AI turns it into a workout; Anthropic doesn't train its models on it and deletes it within 30 days, unless it needs it to investigate abuse or the law requires it.

  • What we keep: never the audio. Whether it came from voice or text and, if you're 18 or over and have seen the notice, the text (what you type or what the voice was transcribed to); and the resulting workout (format, movements and numbers), without your name or account, for the same purposes and period as section 4.4. If you're 18 or over, also what section 4.5 describes. Don't dictate other people's names or details.
  • Daily limit: to limit recordings per day we use the app's random identifier in hashed form, only for that day, and it isn't stored with the audio or the text. We don't use your voice to identify you.
  • Legal basis: providing the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for the stored text and workout, our legitimate interest in improving reading (Art. 6(1)(f)). Cloudflare and Anthropic act as processors, as in section 4.2.
  • Under 14: not available.

4.7 Aggregated, anonymous statistics

We keep your readings, your ratings and app usage data to improve reading and our products. We also use them to produce aggregated, anonymous training statistics (for example, what people train by country or region, and when). Those statistics don't identify you and can't be linked to you: we only release figures that group 10 or more people, with no photos, texts or identifiers. If we ever wanted to use your data in another way, we'll ask you first.

4.8 Suggestions and bugs ("Tell us")

If you use "Tell us", we receive your text, the screenshots you choose, the type of message, the app version, the system and the language. We don't link it to your identifier. We use it only to improve Wodpost and delete it after 90 days. Basis: our legitimate interest in fixing the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If you are under 14, you can only send text.

4.9 Automatic video edit: your clips (only in test versions for iPhone)

This is not in the App Store version or the Google Play version. Making a video from your clips ("Edit it myself" and the AI edit), "Original sound" and video alerts exist only in the test versions of Wodpost for iPhone, the ones installed through TestFlight by invitation. If you have Wodpost from the App Store or Google Play, nothing in this section applies to you: the app uploads no clips, keeps none of their sound and uses no notification code. Putting your card on top of a background video of yours is a different thing: it is in every version and that video never leaves your phone (section 3). If video editing comes to the stores, we will update this policy first.

In those test versions, the clips you choose are uploaded to our service on Cloudflare (processor; see 4.2). Our AI detects the movements and counts the reps to build the video; it does not identify you. Legal basis: providing the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

  • Original clips: deleted about 3 hours after the upload finishes, whether you edit or not (deletion runs periodically, so it can take a little longer, up to 4 hours). If the edit fails because of us, we keep them for that time so you can edit by hand without uploading them again.
  • Edited video and trimmed clips (with AI, or by hand in older test versions, which edited it on our service): we keep them without location or date and, unless you turn on "Original sound", without sound, until you delete them in Settings › "Delete my data", which deletes everything immediately.
  • Original sound (optional): it is off by default. If you turn it on before making the video, we keep the sound of your clips with the trimmed clips and mix it under the music. It can include other people's voices and any music playing in the gym. We only use it to mix it: we don't transcribe it or analyse it to recognise anyone. It is deleted with the video, with "Delete my data". If you are 14 to 17, the option is not available and we keep no sound. Legal basis: providing the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for other people's voices, legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)), with the same safeguards as for their image. If you appear in someone else's video and want us to delete it, write to hola@mosaicolab.app.
  • Video alerts (iPhone only, and only if you allow notifications): we keep with your video your phone's notification code (created by the system; it is not your Apple ID or advertising identifier) and your time zone, only to tell you that your clips are uploaded and not yet edited, that they are about to be deleted, that the upload has stalled, or that your video is ready or has failed. We send at most 2 reminders per video and never between 9:30 pm and 9:00 am in your time zone; the alert that your video is ready or has failed may arrive at night, but silently. Alerts contain no advertising and no details of your workout. The code is deleted when the edit finishes, when you cancel, or when the clips are deleted (about 3 hours after the upload finishes, up to 4); the time zone stays with the video, without identifying you, until you delete it. Alerts go through the Expo notification service (650 Industries, Inc., United States; processor, with the transfer safeguards described in its policy: expo.dev/privacy) and through Apple's. If you turn off the permission in your phone's settings, they stop. Legal basis: providing the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Other people: other people may appear in a gym. Please upload only clips in which you appear or that you have permission to use, and check before publishing the video. We process those images only to build your video, on the basis of legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If you appear in a video and want us to delete it, write to us (section 13).

5. Ads

5.1 When there are ads

  • Rewarded video (your choice): once you've used today's free reading, you can watch a video to read another photo in the cloud, or type the workout in yourself without a video.
  • Full screen: at most one a day, when you come back after sharing a card, and never the first time you share.
  • No ads at all with Premium, or if you're under 14.
  • When an ad appears doesn't depend on any of your data, only on the rules above and your age.

5.2 Who serves them and what they collect

Google (Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC) serves the ads with AdMob and provides the consent form (UMP). Policies: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Inside the app we don't use other ad networks. Measuring our own ads outside the app (TikTok and Meta) is explained in section 5.5.

Google's SDK inside the app, not our code, may collect: Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you're 18 or over and allow it; the identifier for vendors (IDFV); approximate location derived from your IP (never GPS); technical device data; which ads you saw or tapped; and SDK diagnostics.

What our code never sends to Google: your workouts, your photos, your weight, your age or whether you've bought anything. We only tell it the maximum ad content rating (PG) and whether ads must be non-personalized.

5.3 Legal basis, transfers and retention

  • Storing or reading information on your phone for ads: your consent.
  • Personalized ads (18 or over only): your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and, on iPhone, your permission in Apple's prompt (ATT).
  • Non-personalized or limited ads, fraud, frequency capping and security: what you choose in the form, under IAB Europe's TCF.
  • Google may process data in the United States, under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses. Retention is set by Google's policy.

5.4 How you decide

  • 18 or over: you see Google's consent form (in Europe, with "Accept" and "Reject" given equal weight) and then a short screen and Apple's tracking prompt. If you don't allow tracking, the app works the same and ads aren't personalized.
  • 14 to 17: you see Google's form but not Apple's tracking prompt, and your ads are always non-personalized (Art. 28 of the Digital Services Act).
  • You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect what was done before.

5.5 Measuring installs from our ads (Android only, 18 or over)

If you use Wodpost on Android, have said you are 18 or over and have accepted personalized ads in the consent form, the app uses the measurement service AppsFlyer (AppsFlyer Ltd. and affiliates). It tells us whether you installed Wodpost from one of our ads on TikTok or Meta (Instagram and Facebook), so we can stop spending on ads that don't work. If any of that isn't true, the SDK isn't even loaded.

  • What AppsFlyer receives: the Android advertising ID, the Google Play install referrer, IP address, device model and OS version, language, country, app version and an identifier AppsFlyer assigns itself. Only when you install or open the app.
  • What it doesn't receive: your photos, boards, readings, workouts, notes, weight, sex, year of birth, calories, purchases or your age band. We send no events about what you do in the app.
  • Who it's shared with: AppsFlyer acts on our behalf. TikTok and Meta receive or match that data to tell whether the install came from their ad and may process it as independent controllers under their own policies: appsflyer.com/privacy-policy, tiktok.com/legal and facebook.com/privacy/policy. We don't use any of it inside the app (not for ads, offers or difficulty) and we don't sell it.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 22.2 LSSI).
  • Transfers: AppsFlyer, TikTok and Meta may process data outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards described in their policies.
  • How long: as set by AppsFlyer's policy; in our configuration nothing is kept beyond what is needed to measure.
  • Withdrawing: in Settings > "Ad privacy options" (change what you accepted) or by changing your age band. Measurement stops immediately, but what was already sent can't be recalled: to have it deleted, write to hola@mosaicolab.app with your advertising ID (on Android, Settings > Google > Ads) and we'll pass the request on to AppsFlyer. You can also reset that ID on Android.
  • Under 18 and iPhone: not activated. This service isn't used on iPhone.

6. Premium: purchases and subscriptions

Premium removes ads and the Wodpost mark from your cards and gives up to 10 cloud readings a day. It can be an annual subscription, a monthly one or a one-time purchase, at the prices shown by the store.

7-day free trial of the annual plan (the same on iPhone and Android): if you're 18 or over, the app offers it from the day after your first card, never the same day; if you're 14 to 17, it only appears if you open Premium yourself (section 9). If you don't cancel before it ends, the store charges the annual plan; two days before, the app tells you the date and amount and, if you allow it, sends a phone notification. The store manages the trial and the charge: we don't see your payment details.

  • Apple (iPhone) or Google Play (Android) handles payment. We don't see your name, account or payment details (apple.com/legal/privacy, policies.google.com/privacy).
  • The app stores your purchase status and its proof (Apple's signed receipt or the Google Play purchase token) on your phone and checks it with the store, including when you tap "Restore purchases". On Android, the token is sent only to our reading service, which asks Google Play only to confirm your Premium is active; we don't log it, don't use it for anything else and share it with no one except Google Play, which issued it. To limit daily usage we keep, for 24 hours at most, an irreversible fingerprint of the token (not the token). We don't use any third-party purchase service.
  • Cancelling and refunds: in your Apple account settings or in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. The store handles refunds: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com; Google Play from its order history.
  • Legal basis: performing what you bought (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

7. Analytics

We don't use third-party analytics: the app doesn't send usage statistics to anyone. The only exception is the ad-install measurement in section 5.5 (Android only, 18 or over, with consent), which includes nothing about what you do in the app. If we ever send more, we'll say so here first and change the date above.

8. App updates

When it opens, the app may check for a small update on Expo's servers (650 Industries, Inc., United States). That request carries technical data: IP address, platform, app version and a random installation ID. It's used only to download the update. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in the app working and being secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Policy: expo.dev/privacy.

9. Children and age

Wodpost is not directed at children. When you start, we ask for your age band; the answer is required and is stored only on your phone.

  • Under 14: the cloud is not used and no ad or measurement SDK is started: no consent form, no tracking prompt, no ads. You can type your workouts in yourself. No purchases are offered; only "Restore purchases".
  • 14 to 17: cloud reading, always non-personalized ads, no Apple tracking prompt and no install measurement (section 5.5). For purchases, the same plans as 18 or over (annual or monthly subscription, or one-time purchase), with no offers that pop up on their own; if you start the free trial, the app reminds you before the charge (section 6).
  • Ad content: we ask Google for PG at most (may need parental guidance): these are not "for all ages" ads, and Google's filter isn't perfect. If you see something inappropriate, write to us with a screenshot.
  • We comply with applicable child-protection and data-protection laws. If you're a parent or guardian and believe a child's data was processed, write to us.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and port your data, to restrict or object to processing and to withdraw consent (Arts. 15 to 22 GDPR).

  • Data on your phone: you control it, and it's deleted when you delete it in the app or uninstall. Since it doesn't reach us, we can't access or delete it for you.
  • Photos read in the cloud: if you're 18 or over, "Delete my data" in Settings removes the identifier, the area and your details from all of them (section 4.5). To delete a specific photo, tell us the approximate date and time; for what Anthropic keeps up to 30 days, write to us and we'll handle it.
  • Ad data: turn off tracking in iOS settings and, for data already collected, contact Google. For install measurement on Android, see section 5.5.
  • How: write to hola@mosaicolab.app. We reply within one month.
  • Complaints: to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or your country's authority. In the UK, the ICO (ico.org.uk).
  • United States: we don't sell your personal data. On Android, for users 18 or over, sharing your advertising ID with AppsFlyer, TikTok and Meta to measure ads may count as "sharing" for advertising under some state laws; it only happens with your consent and you stop it as section 5.5 says. Users under 18 never see personalized ads, and the app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

11. Security

We have no accounts and don't keep your name with your workouts. The photo travels encrypted (HTTPS), our service doesn't keep your IP and what we keep (section 4.4) is encrypted at rest in Cloudflare storage (AES-256), carries no location or photo metadata, and is only visible to our team. App data is protected by your phone's security.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change something important, we'll update this page, change the date above and, if your consent is needed, ask again in the app.
