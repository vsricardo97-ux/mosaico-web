How to add a WOD in Wodpost

Five steps and about 20 seconds: from the box whiteboard to a saved WOD and a ready-to-share card. Works the same on Android and iPhone.

  1. Tap “New workout”

    On the home screen, tap New workout. A sheet opens to pick where the WOD comes from.

    New workout

  2. Choose “Photo of the board”

    Under “Where is it from?” tap Photo of the board. The first time, it explains what happens to the photo and asks you to accept. Already have it in your camera roll, or a screenshot from your box's app? Use From your photos. No board? By hand.

    Where is it from?
    Photo of the board
    From your photos
    By hand

  3. Crop the board

    Move the frame and drag the corners until only your workout is inside. If other people's names show up, keep them outside the frame.

    Drag to resize the frame

  4. Wait for the read, then check it

    This is the cropped photo Wodpost reads. In a few seconds it turns it into a WOD: the strength work (pause back squat 5×5 at 55%, dumbbell deadlift 5×15) and a 16-minute EMOM with its four movements. Anything it is unsure about is flagged in orange in the editor: double-tap the text to fix it. A misread weight changes the workout, so it is worth five seconds.

    Cropped photo, ready to read

  5. Save or share your card

    And this is the card that comes out of that board: 16 MIN · EMOM big, the strength work on top, the four movements and an estimated calorie range. Tap to change the style; the background can be your photo, a colour or a video. Tap Save to keep it in your history or Share to send it to your Instagram story or Photos.

Tips for a better read

  • Stand square-on and avoid the glare of the lights.
  • Fit the whole board in, but close enough to read every line.
  • If your box posts the WOD as a screenshot, that works too.
