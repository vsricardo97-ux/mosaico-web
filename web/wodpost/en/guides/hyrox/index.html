Hyrox: stations and how to log it

Hyrox is a fitness race with the same format worldwide, so you can compare your time with anyone else’s. It’s running and working, eight times over.

The format

You run 1 km and then do a functional exercise station. That repeats eight times, for 8 km of running and 8 stations. There are singles, doubles and relay categories, with different weights depending on the category.

The 8 stations, in order

  1. SkiErg, 1,000 m
  2. Sled push, 50 m
  3. Sled pull, 50 m
  4. Burpee broad jumps, 80 m
  5. Rowing, 1,000 m
  6. Farmers carry, 200 m
  7. Sandbag lunges, 100 m
  8. Wall balls, 100 reps

What to log

  • Your total time and, if you can, each run and station split.
  • Your category and the weights you used.
  • The station where you lost the most time: that’s your easiest gain.

Training for Hyrox at your box

Many boxes program sessions with running, rowing, sled work or wall balls. When the board shows a Hyrox-style workout, Wodpost reads it like any other and saves it to your library so you can compare one week with another, and your card tells you how much you’ve improved.
