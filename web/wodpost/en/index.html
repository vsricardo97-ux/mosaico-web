Only against yourself

Wodpost remembers your marks and puts what matters on the card: “0:42 faster” than last time, your new 1RM or your first Fran. With the classic benchmark WODs: Fran, Grace, Cindy or Murph.

Say it instead of typing it

No whiteboard? Dictate the workout by voice or type it, check it, and Wodpost gets it ready for your card. It counts as one of the day’s readings.

Your month and weeks done

Wodpost sums up your month at the box: what you loaded and what to stretch. If you set a weekly goal, it counts the weeks you hit it. No nudges or alerts: look whenever you like.

No accounts, no sign-up

Your workouts stay on your phone. There’s no sign-up or password: your library, your records and your weight, if you enter it for calories, live on your phone and you delete them whenever you want.

To read the whiteboard, Wodpost sends the downsized photo, without location, to a cloud AI (Claude, by Anthropic). We keep the photo, what was read and your rating to improve the reading, without your name or account (if you are 18 or over, with a random app identifier and your approximate area, which you can delete in Settings). Anthropic only reads it: it doesn’t train on it and deletes its copy within 30 days at most. The app explains this before your first reading, and under 14 there is no cloud reading: you type the workout by hand.

FAQ

Do I need Instagram?

No. You can save the card to Photos or send it on WhatsApp or anywhere. The Instagram button just saves you a step.

Does it read handwriting?

Yes, including marker and scribbles. When it isn’t sure, it flags it so you can tap it. And it doesn’t have to be a whiteboard: a screenshot from your box’s or training app works too, and so does a photo of a computer screen.

Which workouts does it understand?

For time, AMRAP, EMOM, rounds, chippers, strength with sets and 1RM, and the mix of parts in a normal class: warm-up, strength and WOD.

Is there an Android version?

Yes, it’s already on Google Play. The iPhone version isn’t on the App Store yet: you can try the test version on TestFlight or leave your email below and we’ll tell you the day it’s out.

Does it count calories?

Only if you want, and they’re off by default. It’s a rough figure (the average of a range, shown with “~” and “est.”) from your weight and work time, not a measurement or medical advice.

No whiteboard?

Dictate the workout by voice or type it, and Wodpost turns it into a workout. Your voice is turned into text and isn’t kept; the text goes to Anthropic’s AI and is deleted within 30 days at most.

Does it make videos?

Yes, if you’re 18 or over. Upload the clips you filmed while training: the AI picks your best reps, cuts them to the beat and puts your card on top. You can tweak it if you like. It costs 3 credits, one a week; with Premium you get 4 a month.

What are credits?

If you’re 14 or over, you earn 1 credit each day you save your WOD and another for each short video you choose to watch, and you keep up to 3. An extra reading costs 1; a Premium style, 3, and an AI video, 3: one a week of each. With Premium you don’t need them.
